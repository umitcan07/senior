# Nounce — Voice Recording Brief

Thanks for recording for us! This brief has everything you need: the
recording specs, the **25 sentences**, and the **44 single words**. Please
read the specs first, then record each item in the two tables below.

(Internal note: sentences mirror `doc/validation_sentences.md`; words mirror
`IPA_AUDIO_MAP` in `app/src/routes/learn.tsx`. Cross-refs: #25, #44, #48.)

## Recording specs

**Audio format**
- **WAV**, **mono**, **16 kHz or 48 kHz**, **16-bit PCM**.
- **No effects** — no compression, EQ, de-noising, normalization, or reverb.
- Quiet room, consistent mic distance, minimal background noise.

**Delivery**
- One file per item, flat (all files in a single folder).
- **Natural, conversational** delivery — as if speaking to a friend. Please
  do **not** over-articulate, slow down, or use a "newsreader" voice.
- One clean take per item is enough; re-record if there's a slip or noise.
- Record in your own natural accent throughout — please keep it consistent
  across the whole set.
